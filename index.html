01 · About

Curious about dynamic 3D worlds.

I am a doctoral candidate at the CAMP Computer Vision Lab at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), supervised by Federico Tombari and Stefano Gasperini. My research focuses on dynamic 3D reconstruction and world models. I also completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at TUM, specializing in Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and Autonomous Driving.

Outside research, I enjoy road cycling and beach volleyball in summer, indoor volleyball and dancing Boogie Woogie throughout the rest of the year.
